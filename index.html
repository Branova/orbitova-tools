Install in a minute

The app is free and not yet code-signed, so your computer asks once before opening it.

🖥 Mac

  1. Download and open the .zip, then drag Orbitova Downloader into Applications.
  2. First time only: right-click the app → Open, then Open again.
    Or: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Paste links and press Download.

⊞ Windows

  1. Download the .zip, right-click it → Extract All.
  2. Open the folder and double-click Orbitova Downloader.exe.
  3. If you see "Windows protected your PC": More info → Run anyway. Only once.

Questions

 

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no ads, no watermark. It's a free tool from the Orbitova team for the creator community.

Which Macs does it work on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 12 Monterey or later.

A download says "Needs login". What do I do?

Log into that site in your browser, then choose the browser under "Use login from browser" and try again. On Windows, Firefox works best for this.

Why does my computer warn me when I first open it?

The app isn't code-signed yet, which costs a yearly fee. The warning is standard for unsigned apps and only appears the first time.

What can I download?

Only content you own or have permission to use, such as your own uploads, client footage you've been given access to, or openly licensed material. You're responsible for following copyright law and each platform's terms.
